# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project free from harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, accept responsibility, focus on the project, and protect other people's privacy. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method in the repository profile. Maintainers will review reports fairly, preserve confidentiality as practical, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants according to severity and pattern. Retaliation is prohibited.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ under CC BY 4.0.
